Older lab news, 2012 to 2018. [Back to recent news](./).

## 2018

- **September 2018.** Lab cookout and campout.
- **July 2018.** [RADCamp](https://radcamp.github.io/AF-Biota/) SNP analysis workshop at the Universidade de São Paulo, taught by Isaac Overcast, Laura Bertola, and Mariana Vasconcellos, followed a month later by [RADCamp NYC](https://radcamp.github.io/NYC2018/) at Columbia, taught by Isaac Overcast, Deren Eaton, and Sandra Hoffberg.
- **July 2018.** Welcome to new PhD student Connor French, master's student Andrea Calderon Brito, and undergraduate Casey Sniffin.

## 2017

- **November 2017.** Welcome to new PhD student Melina Giakoumis, and to visiting PhD student Victor Garcia-Olivares from Brent Emerson's lab at IPNA in La Laguna, Spain.
- **May 2017.** Welcome to visiting PhD student Pedro Taucce from Celio Haddad's lab at Unesp, Rio Claro, Brazil, who uses acoustic and molecular data to delimit cryptic frog species in the Atlantic Forest.
- **May 2017.** Methods paper for Xander Xue's R package [Multi-DICE](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.12686), for comparative population genomic inference under hierarchical co-demographic models, in *Molecular Ecology Resources*.
- **April 2017.** Funded working group at iDiv with Rosemary Gillespie: "sEcoEvo: Biodiversity Dynamics, the Nexus Between Space & Time."
- **February 2017.** Welcome to postdoc Laura Bertola, who will work on comparative adaptive genomics.
- **February 2017.** Welcome to visiting PhD student Vanessa Bonatti from Tiago Mauricio Francoy's lab at the Universidade de São Paulo, who uses RAD data to infer the demographic history of the stingless bee *Melipona subnitida*.
- **February 2017.** [Sasquatch turns 100!](http://lozierlab.ua.edu/news/sasquatch-turns-100) Thanks to Jeff Lozier for the link.
- **January 2017.** Paper by Sara Lipshutz, Isaac Overcast, Elizabeth Derryberry, and Robb Brumfield on [behavioral response to song and genetic divergence in two subspecies of white-crowned sparrows](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.14002), in *Molecular Ecology*.

## 2016

- **October 2016.** Joint Burbrink, Brian Smith, and hickerlab paper on [demographic responses to Pleistocene climate change in Eastern Nearctic vertebrates](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.12695), in *Ecology Letters*.
- **October 2016.** Welcome to visiting PhD student Natali Hurtado Miranda from Guillermo D'Elía's lab at the Universidad Austral de Chile, who studies the role of Andean rivers in the speciation of sigmodontine rodents.
- **October 2016.** Ed Myers's paper on [asynchronous diversification of snakes in the North American warm deserts](https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.12873), in the *Journal of Biogeography*; the first joint Burbrink and hickerlab effort.
- **October 2016.** Inaugural joint lab meeting of the Munshi-South and Hickerson groups at the Fordham field station, with talks, a BBQ, and a mushroom hunt.
- **September 2016.** Champak Reddy's ABLE preprint on bioRxiv: [approximate likelihood inference of complex population histories and recombination from multiple genomes](https://doi.org/10.1101/077958).
- **August 2016.** Farewell Korean BBQ for Roberta Damasceno, heading back to the Universidade de São Paulo, and Tyler Joseph, starting his PhD at Columbia.
- **August 2016.** One-day "jam session" of talks with part of Scott Edwards's lab.
- **July 2016.** Ivan Prates et al. in *PNAS*, a joint Carnaval and Hickerson lab effort, on [inferring lizard responses to climate dynamics from historical demography in neotropical forests](https://www.pnas.org/content/113/29/7978.full).
- **July 2016.** Welcome to visiting PhD student Gregory Thom from Cristina Miyaki's lab at the Universidade de São Paulo, here for a year.
- **June 2016.** Lab alum John Robinson is now an assistant professor at Michigan State University.
- **May 2016.** Undergraduate Tyler Joseph's paper, ["Demographic inference under a spatially continuous coalescent model,"](https://doi.org/10.1038/hdy.2016.28) in *Heredity*.
- **April 2016.** Paper by Stephen Harris and lab members on [how urbanization shaped the demographic history of white-footed mice in New York City](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsbl.2015.0983), in *Biology Letters*; covered by the LA Times.
- **March 2016.** Tyler Joseph will start doctoral studies at Columbia University with Itsik Pe'er and Yaniv Erlich.
- **January 2016.** Brown et al., with the Carnaval, Franks, and Hickerson labs, on [predicting the genetic consequences of future climate change](http://www.amjbot.org/content/103/1/153.short) by combining spatial demography, the coalescent, and landscape history, in the *American Journal of Botany*.

## 2015

- **December 2015.** Two Stephen Harris preprints covered by *The Molecular Ecologist*.
- **November 2015.** Xander Xue's paper on [the aggregate site frequency spectrum (aSFS) for comparative population genomic inference](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.13447), in *Molecular Ecology*.
- **October 2015.** Welcome to visiting PhD student Sara Lipshutz from the Derryberry lab at Tulane, working on the demographic history of West Coast white-crowned sparrow subspecies.
- **September 2015.** Lab retreat and BBQ to send off Diego and welcome Roberta, including a mushroom hunt where Roberta found a 5 lb *Hericium* that we cooked and ate.
- **September 2015.** Terry Demos's paper on [comparative population genomics of Eastern Afromontane small mammals and Quaternary environmental change](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0131800), in *PLoS ONE*.
- **September 2015.** Diego Alvarado-Serrano's paper on [spatially explicit summary statistics for historical population genetic inference](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12489), in *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*.
- **September 2015.** Welcome to Roberta Damasceno, visiting postdoc from the Universidade de São Paulo, who studies hybridization and reproductive isolation in Atlantic Forest lizards.
- **July 2015.** Talks by Xander Xue and Diego Alvarado-Serrano at the Evolution meeting in Guarujá, Brazil.
- **June 2015.** Lab alum Katriina Ilves is now an assistant professor at Pace University.
- **April 2015.** Three travel awards for Isaac Overcast: the Evolution meeting in Brazil, the SSB standalone meeting at the University of Michigan, and the Summer Institute in Statistical Genetics at the University of Washington.
- **March 2015.** Isaac Overcast's talk "Patterns of Trance across the Tree of Life" at the CUNY Conference on Trance.
- **January 2015.** Lab road trip to Louisiana, with visits to the Gilchrist, Derryberry, Blum, Richards-Zawacki, Lozier, Esselstyn, Brown, and Miller labs.
- **January 2015.** JT Boehm's paper showing that seahorses (*Hippocampus erectus*) on the western mid-Atlantic coast are [residents, not vagrants](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0116219), in *PLoS ONE*.

## 2013

- **December 2013.** John Robinson moves to a permanent Research Scientist position at the South Carolina Department of Natural Resources.
- **October 2013.** Two papers from Terry Demos: cryptic diversity among small mammals of the Eastern Afromontane hotspot, in *Molecular Phylogenetics and Evolution*, and the taxonomy of *Hylomyscus*, including a new species from western Kenya, in the *Journal of Mammalogy*.
- **September 2013.** Five-year NSF/NASA Dimensions of Biodiversity grant, "Dimensions US-BIOTA-São Paulo," for biodiversity prediction in the Brazilian Atlantic Forest, led by Ana Carnaval with collaborators at CCNY, the New York Botanical Garden, and the Universidade de São Paulo (FAPESP side led by Cristina Miyaki).
- **August 2013.** Welcome to postdoc Diego Alvarado-Serrano, PhD from the University of Michigan with Lacey Knowles.
- **June 2013.** The lab attended the SSMPG 2013 workshop in Aussois, France.
- **May 2013.** JT Boehm's paper on marine dispersal and barriers driving Atlantic seahorse diversification, in the *Journal of Biogeography*.
- **April 2013.** Welcome to Ana Christina Fazza, PhD student with Cristina Miyaki at the Universidade de São Paulo, who will develop demographic models for Atlantic Forest birds.
- **March 2013.** NSF CAREER award [DEB-1253710](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=1253710), "Dynamic models of isolation and admixture for community-scale population genomic inference."
- **February 2013.** John Robinson's paper on [approximate Bayesian estimation of extinction rate in the Finnish *Daphnia magna* metapopulation](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.12283), in *Molecular Ecology*.
